Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I play Pocket Cue?

A: Aim your shots, adjust the power and use cue ball spin to plan your next move. Start with the tutorials and practice challenges to get familiar with the controls and improve your shots.

Q: Which pool modes are available?

A: Pocket Cue includes Eight Ball, Nine Ball and Ten Ball. Try the different modes to vary your matches and practice.

Q: Can I play against the computer?

A: Yes. Computer opponents have different difficulty levels, so you can choose a level that suits your experience and try a harder opponent as you improve.

Q: Can I play with another person?

A: Yes. Two players can take turns playing a match on the same device.

Q: Does the game require an internet connection?

A: Pocket Cue supports offline gameplay. The game contains ads, and an internet connection is required to load advertisements.

Q: Can I adjust the sound and controls?

A: Yes. Use the game's settings to adjust sound and control options to suit your preferences.

Q: The game is lagging or crashing. What should I do?

A: Check for an available game update, close and reopen the app, and restart your device. Closing other apps and freeing up storage may also help. If the issue continues, email us your device model, operating system version, game version and the steps that caused the problem.

Q: Where can I read the privacy policy?

A: Read the Pocket Cue Privacy Policy for information about data and privacy.

Helpful Tips

  • Plan your next shot: Think about where the cue ball will stop after contact, as well as which ball you want to pocket.
  • Control your power: Use only as much power as the shot needs. Softer shots can make cue ball positioning easier.
  • Practice cue ball spin: Try different spin settings in practice to learn how they affect the cue ball's path and your next shot.
  • Check the angles: Take time to line up the object ball with the pocket and watch for balls blocking its path.
  • Protect the cue ball: Consider its path after impact to reduce the chance of a scratch.
  • Build your skills: Use tutorials and practice challenges, then try computer opponents at different difficulty levels.
