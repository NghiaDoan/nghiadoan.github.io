Latest Update: September 4, 2026

Blokyra: Block Puzzle Arcade is a mobile block-puzzle arcade for iOS and Android. The app may expand with additional game experiences over time. The app does not require a user account, and the developer does not ask you to submit your name, phone number, postal address, or other direct personal identifiers in order to play.

Summary: Gameplay progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Google AdMob may process data to provide advertisements, and Apple App Store / Google Play handle purchases and restoration. Blokyra does not currently provide account-based cloud save.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Blokyra stores information needed to operate the game locally on your device, including:

  • Language preference, BGM volume, SFX volume, and mute setting.
  • Current resumable run state for supported games.
  • Scores, best scores, rescue usage, and other deterministic gameplay state required to resume correctly.
  • Local entitlement/cache information for the Remove Ads purchase and the timestamp used to enforce interstitial-ad frequency limits.

This local gameplay/save data is not uploaded to a Blokyra account or cloud-save service because the app currently does not provide one. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may remove locally stored progress and settings.

2. Advertising and Consent

Blokyra uses Google AdMob for rewarded advertisements used by the Rescue feature and for interstitial advertisements that may be shown after an eligible Quit action.

Depending on your platform, region, consent choices, and device settings, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers where permitted (for example IDFA on iOS or AAID on Android).
  • IP address, device/app information, operating-system information, diagnostics, and advertising interaction data.
  • Approximate location inferred from network information.

Where required, the app may present privacy/consent choices before requesting ads. On iOS, App Tracking Transparency permission may also be requested when applicable. Refusing tracking permission does not prevent core gameplay, although advertising may be less personalized.

If the Remove Ads entitlement is active, Blokyra skips the rewarded-ad requirement for Rescue and does not intentionally show the Quit interstitial placements covered by that entitlement.

3. In-App Purchases

Blokyra offers a one-time, non-consumable Remove Ads purchase. Purchases and restores are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details.

The app may store a local entitlement cache for faster UI behavior, but the corresponding app store remains the authority for purchase ownership and restoration.

4. Third-Party Services

Blokyra may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob and Google consent/privacy services for advertising.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit for iOS purchases and restoration.
  • Google Play / Play Billing for Android purchases and restoration.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Data Retention and Security

Local game data generally remains on your device until it is overwritten, cleared, or removed with the app. Data processed by third-party providers is retained according to their own policies. We use reasonable measures to keep app data limited to what is needed for gameplay, settings, ads, and purchases, but no electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, SDKs, legal requirements, or store requirements change. Updates become effective when the revised policy is posted on this page.
